# [Unit 2.8] Related Rates
# Textbook Alignment: Thomas' Calculus 14th Ed, Section 3.8

## Lesson Plan
### Objective
To solve "Related Rates" problems by identifying variables, relating them through geometry or physics equations, and differentiating with respect to time ($t$) using the Chain Rule.

### Key Concepts (Thomas-Verbatim)
1. **The Concept**: Determining how the rate of change of one quantity affects the rate of change of another.
2. **Strategy**:
   - Draw a picture (Step 1).
   - Write down numerical info (Step 2).
   - Identify what to find (Step 3).
   - Write a relating equation (Step 4).
   - Differentiate with respect to $t$ (Step 5).
   - Evaluate (Step 6).
3. **Common Models**:
   - **Pythagorean Theorem**: $x^2 + y^2 = s^2$.
   - **Cone Volume**: $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$.
   - **Sphere Volume**: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.
   - **Trigonometric Relations**: $\tan \theta = y/x$.

### Examples (Thomas-Precise)
- **Example 1 (Conical Tank)**: Water fills at 9 ft$^3$/min. At $y=6$, the water level rises at $1/\pi \approx 0.32$ ft/min.
- **Example 2 (Balloon)**: A balloon rises 150m away. At $\theta = \pi/4$ and $d\theta/dt = 0.14$, the balloon rises at 42 m/min.
- **Example 3 (Police Chase)**: A cruiser (0.6 mi N) chases a car (0.8 mi E). Distant $s$ increases at 20 mph, cruiser speed 60 mph $\implies$ car speed 70 mph.

## Storyboard (Doraemon Edition)
**Topic: The Chain of Change**

### Panel 1: The Balloon Pump (The Sphere)
- **Visual**: Doraemon is pumping air into a spherical balloon. A holographic display shows $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$. 
- **Dialogue**:
  - **Doraemon**: "Notice, Nobita! As the volume grows, the radius grows too. Their rates are linked by the Chain Rule: $dV/dt = 4\pi r^2 (dr/dt)$!"
  - **Nobita**: "So if I know how fast the air goes in, I can find how fast the balloon is expanding!"

### Panel 2: The Conical Crisis (Example 1)
- **Visual**: Water is leaking into a giant conical cup. Doraemon uses a "Similar Triangle" gadget to show $r/h = 5/10$.
- **Dialogue**:
  - **Doraemon**: "This tank is tricky! We need to eliminate the radius $r$ by using the shape of the cone. $V = (\pi/12)h^3$. Now we can track the rising water level!"

### Panel 3: The Balloon Tracker (Example 2)
- **Visual**: Nobita is holding a "Range Finder" tracking a hot air balloon rising straight up. An angle $\theta$ glows on the screen.
- **Dialogue**:
  - **Doraemon**: "The balloon is too high to measure with a tape! But we can track the angle. Since $y = 150 \tan \theta$, we can find the vertical speed just by watching the angle change!"

### Panel 4: The High-Speed Chase (Example 3)
- **Visual**: A police car coming from the North and a speeding car going East. A "Radar" shows the distance $s$ between them.
- **Dialogue**:
  - **Nobita**: "It's a right-angled triangle! $s^2 = x^2 + y^2$!"
  - **Doraemon**: "Exactly! Differentiate with respect to time: $2s(ds/dt) = 2x(dx/dt) + 2y(dy/dt)$. Even without seeing the speedometer, the radar tells all!"

### Panel 5: The Pulley Problem (Example 6)
- **Visual**: A worker pulling a rope through a pulley to lift a weight (Figure 3.38). Doraemon points to the fixed length of the rope.
- **Dialogue**:
  - **Doraemon**: "The rope length is constant, but the triangle is changing! As the worker walks away, the height of the weight rises. Every move is connected by Geometry!"

### Panel 6: The Strategy Scroll
- **Visual**: Doraemon holds a scroll with the 6 steps: 1. Picture, 2. Info, 3. Find, 4. Equation, 5. Differentiate, 6. Solve.
- **Dialogue**:
  - **Doraemon**: "Related rates are just like a puzzle, Nobita. Follow the steps, relate the variables, and let the Chain Rule do the heavy lifting!"
  - **Nobita**: "I'm ready for the next challenge!"
