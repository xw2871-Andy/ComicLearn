# Unit 3.1: Extrema on an Interval

## Pedagogical Cycle
**Context -> Definition -> Theorems -> Example regarding Theorems -> Theorems -> Example**

## Scene 1: The Giant Dorayaki Contest (Context)
**BGM:** Cheerful, bustling, domestic kitchen music.
**Visual:** Nobita is in his home kitchen wearing an apron, staring at a futuristic "Quantum Oven." Inside is a massive, slowly baking Dorayaki (red bean pancake). The oven's heat dial goes from $[-1, 2]$. Doraemon is drooling beside him. The holographic recipe shows the Fluffiness Score function: $f(x) = 3x^4 - 4x^3$.
**Audio:**
*   **Nobita:** "Doraemon, I am baking a giant Dorayaki for the neighborhood contest!"
*   **Doraemon:** "The Quantum Oven's heat dial only goes from negative one to two."
*   **Nobita:** "The recipe says the fluffiness score is three x to the fourth, minus four x cubed!"
*   **Doraemon:** "We need to find the absolute maximum fluffiness score on that closed interval!"
*   **Nobita:** "I will just crank the dial all the way up to two! Hotter is better, right?"
*   **Doraemon:** "Not always! What if the perfect temperature is somewhere in the middle?"

## Scene 2: The Extreme Value Theorem Scanner (Definition)
**BGM:** Analytical, puzzle-solving gadget sound.
**Visual:** Doraemon pulls out a glowing, rectangular device called the "Extreme Value Theorem Scanner." He scans the oven. A green holographic checkmark appears, confirming the function is continuous.
**Audio:**
*   **Doraemon:** "Let us use the Extreme Value Theorem Scanner!"
*   **Nobita:** "A scanner? What does it do?"
*   **Doraemon:** "It guarantees that a continuous function on a closed interval has an absolute maximum and minimum!"
*   **Nobita:** "So the highest score could be at the endpoints, or somewhere inside at a peak?"
*   **Doraemon:** "Exactly! To find the peaks inside, we have to find the critical numbers!"
*   **Nobita:** "Critical numbers? That sounds like a job for the derivative!"

## Scene 3: Differentiating the Fluffiness (Theorems)
**BGM:** Mathematical "Eureka" chime.
**Visual:** Nobita writes glowing math equations in the air with his finger. He uses the Power Rule on $f(x) = 3x^4 - 4x^3$. The numbers drop down and multiply. The new hologram reads $f'(x) = 12x^3 - 12x^2$.
**Audio:**
*   **Nobita:** "I will take the derivative of the fluffiness function!"
*   **Doraemon:** "Use the Power Rule on three x to the fourth, minus four x cubed!"
*   **Nobita:** "Four times three is twelve... so twelve x cubed!"
*   **Doraemon:** "Minus three times four... minus twelve x squared!"
*   **Nobita:** "So f prime of x is twelve x cubed minus twelve x squared!"
*   **Doraemon:** "Perfect! Now we set the derivative equal to zero to find the flat peaks and valleys!"

## Scene 4: Factoring the Critical Numbers (Example regarding Theorems)
**BGM:** Steady, rhythmic algebra music.
**Visual:** The holographic equation $12x^3 - 12x^2 = 0$ floats above the oven. Nobita mathematically pulls out the common factor $12x^2$, leaving $12x^2(x - 1) = 0$. The roots $x = 0$ and $x = 1$ light up in gold.
**Audio:**
*   **Nobita:** "Setting twelve x cubed minus twelve x squared equal to zero..."
*   **Doraemon:** "You need to factor it, Nobita! Pull out the greatest common factor!"
*   **Nobita:** "I can pull out twelve x squared! That leaves twelve x squared times the quantity x minus one, equals zero!"
*   **Doraemon:** "So what are the critical numbers inside our interval?"
*   **Nobita:** "x equals zero, and x equals one! Those are our turning points!"
*   **Doraemon:** "Now we must test them against the endpoints!"

## Scene 5: The Candidates Test Table (Theorems)
**BGM:** Tense, comparative calculation music.
**Visual:** Doraemon projects a glowing "Candidates Test Table" hologram. It lists $x = -1, 0, 1, 2$. Nobita plugs the numbers back into the original fluffiness formula $f(x) = 3x^4 - 4x^3$. The scores fill in: $7, 0, -1, 16$.
**Audio:**
*   **Doraemon:** "I will project the Candidates Test Table hologram!"
*   **Nobita:** "We have four candidates for the heat dial: negative one, zero, one, and two!"
*   **Doraemon:** "Plug them into the original fluffiness function, not the derivative!"
*   **Nobita:** "At negative one, the score is seven. At zero, the score is zero."
*   **Doraemon:** "Keep going! What about at exactly one?"
*   **Nobita:** "At one, the score is negative one... that's a burnt Dorayaki! And at two, the score is sixteen!"

## Scene 6: The Golden Dorayaki (Final Example)
**BGM:** Epic, joyful, mouth-watering victory music.
**Visual:** Nobita turns the Quantum Oven dial firmly to $2$. The oven dings, opening to reveal a massive, perfectly golden, impossibly fluffy Dorayaki glowing with a divine light. Doraemon is starry-eyed and drooling waterfalls.
**Audio:**
*   **Doraemon:** "Look at the table! The absolute maximum fluffiness score is sixteen!"
*   **Nobita:** "And it happens when the heat dial is set to exactly two!"
*   **Doraemon:** "So your first guess was right, but now we have mathematical proof!"
*   **Nobita:** "I am turning the dial to two! The giant Dorayaki is perfectly golden and fluffy!"
*   **Doraemon:** "Absolute maximum deliciousness achieved! Let us eat!"
*   **Nobita:** "Calculus makes the best desserts in the neighborhood!"
