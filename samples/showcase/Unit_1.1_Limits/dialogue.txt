Nobita
Doraemon, I dropped this rock off the cliff, but how fast is it falling right now? Exactly at two seconds?

Doraemon
That is the perfect question for Calculus, Nobita! To find the speed at an exact instant, we first need to understand average speed. 

Nobita
Average speed? Like total distance divided by total time?

Doraemon
Exactly. If the distance the rock falls is y equals sixteen t squared, its average speed between two and three seconds is eighty feet per second. But that is just an average.

Nobita
Right, it is speeding up the whole time! So how do we get the exact speed at two seconds?

Doraemon
We shrink the time interval! Instead of one whole second, we look at a tiny interval, h. We calculate the average speed between two and two plus h.

Nobita
Oh, I see! So the formula becomes sixteen times two plus h squared, minus sixteen times two squared, all over h.

Doraemon
You are a genius today, Nobita! When we simplify that, we get sixty four plus sixteen h. And as that tiny time interval h approaches zero, the speed becomes exactly sixty four feet per second! That is the instantaneous rate of change.
